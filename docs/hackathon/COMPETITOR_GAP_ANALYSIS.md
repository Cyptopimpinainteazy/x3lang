The largest competitive risk is that contract-custodied scoped-agent spending is already offered by multiple entrants. X3's typed policy/compiler, authenticated quote binding and independently inspectable proof pipeline must demonstrate additional value; the existence of a spending cap or browser attack preview alone is not originality.
