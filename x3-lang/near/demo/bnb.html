What remains before one funded transaction

  1. Choose a user-controlled BSC wallet with a verified refund address.
  2. Verify a user-controlled NEAR recipient is active and can receive the intended token.
  3. Obtain a fresh acceptable quote. The latest dry request is currently blocked by a provider minimum; never increase funds automatically to satisfy it.
  4. Bind the exact program, token, amount, recipient, slippage, fee and nonce to Guard authorization.
  5. Show the complete terms and obtain explicit owner approval before signing/broadcasting.
  6. Collect genuine BSC transaction inclusion, 1Click status, NEAR delivery evidence and a verified receipt.
